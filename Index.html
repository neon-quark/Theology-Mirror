A Self-Examination in Christian Thought

The Theology Mirror

What do you really believe?

This questionnaire is not a test of orthodoxy — it is an invitation to honest self-examination. Many beliefs we hold were never consciously chosen. They were absorbed through sermons, worship songs, family traditions, and the water we swim in as modern American Christians.

Some of those absorbed beliefs have names — and histories. Many were debated fiercely in the early church and eventually recognized as departing from what Scripture actually teaches. Most people who hold them have no idea they have a name, let alone that they were examined and addressed centuries ago.

This questionnaire will help you identify twelve theological tendencies that quietly shape the faith of many modern Christians without their awareness.

You will be asked 55 questions in random order. Answer each one based on your honest instinct rather than what you think the correct theological answer should be. The most revealing answers come from your gut, not your head.

There are no trick questions and no wrong people — only unexamined beliefs. The goal is not to shame but to illuminate.
